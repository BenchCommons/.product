# AGENTS.md — .product

Follow the org-wide rules in [BenchCommons/.github AGENTS.md](https://github.com/BenchCommons/.github/blob/main/AGENTS.md).

## Role
Org product governance: `ROADMAP.md` (initiatives and their order), `CONTRACTS.md` (each contract between repos and its owner) and `docs/adr/` (cross-repo decisions, Nygard format).

## Rules
- Only cross-repo content goes here. Per-repo specs stay in each repo's `docs/specs/`.
- A contract is listed here but specified in its owner repo. Never copy a contract's text into this repo.
- ADRs: `docs/adr/NNNN-kebab-title.md` with Context, Decision, Status and Consequences. Changing an accepted decision takes a new ADR that supersedes it.
